Hello!

I’m Minseok Kim, studying Applied Artificial Intelligence at Sungkyunkwan University (B.S., M.S., Ph.D. track). I build dependable ML systems at the intersection of multimodal AI and digital therapeutics.

My work spans Natural Language Processing, Computer Vision, and Audio, with applications in mental health, ophthalmology, and audiology. I care about practical research: robust evaluation, clear reasoning, and responsible deployment.

Research Interests

Multimodal
Learning representations and reasoning across text, vision, and audio—designed for robustness, alignment, and reliable generalization in real-world settings.
Natural Language Processing Computer Vision Audio
Digital Therapeutics
Building clinically meaningful AI that is safe by design—grounded in evidence, sensitive to bias, and evaluated with rigorous, transparent protocols.
Mental Health Ophthalmology Audiology

Projects

MIND

A multimodal digital therapeutics direction focusing on reliable sensing/understanding and intervention-ready outputs. Emphasis on robustness, human-centered evaluation, and safety constraints.

BiasQA

A bias-aware QA and evaluation pipeline: constructing challenging prompts, measuring stereotype leakage, and designing mitigation strategies (prompting + evaluation) with reproducible reporting.
